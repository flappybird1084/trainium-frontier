## This session's verdict: 0.96 is NOT reachable on this line; v46 is the ceiling. All leads closed with device evidence.

### Step-time / throughput (the real gap to the leaders is ~2x tokens; we can't get them)
- Step = 0.52s = fwd_bwd 0.416 + grad_sync 0.044 + opt 0.056. Profile (`mfu-profile-2026-09-21.md`): compiled graphs
  already ~25% MFU, **Vector-bound** (backward Vector ~100% of wall; TENSOR_TENSOR 132ms). Only ~25ms of the step is
  reducible (activation-DMA round-trips); the rest is real matmul (224ms), compiler-balanced Vector, and irreducible
  comm/optimizer. **0.42s needs -100ms that does not exist.**
- CLOSED with device tests: GpSimd engine-hint for tensor_tensor **fails to compile** (bf16+fp32); `_coalescing_manager`
  **unimplemented on the neuron backend**; compiler-fused layer (LAYER=torch) is **5x SLOWER** (2.69s) and OOMs at DB16;
  optimizer/comm overlap impossible (lever F: runtime serializes collectives+compute); fp8 not matmul-bound + won't
  compile; batch scaling linear; depth 10 faster but +0.0044, depth 14 slower+worse.
- Megakernel moonshot: DEAD ON ARRIVAL (Stage-1 code review). Vendor `_attention_cte` is HBM-bound by construction (Q from
  HBM, output to HBM, no SBUF path; 64 training seqs = 48MB > 24MB SBUF; bwd needs HBM anyway). Own flash-attention is the
  only SBUF route and it NaN'd historically (rule #11) / parity-at-best. Not worth it.

### Quality levers (we are token-limited, so any added compute steals tokens and loses at fixed time)
