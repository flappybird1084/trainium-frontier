## Rules learned this session (also in memory)
- **600 s screens over-favor high-LR configs and do NOT transfer to the full gate** — never promote an LR change on a screen
  win alone; gate it (v41 lesson).
- Flow/DeltaNet params must NOT inherit the boosted SCALAR_LR — give them their own optimizer group at a small LR (~0.01),
  or they diverge in the first handful of steps.
- Fused compiler graphs of small-matrix chains (DeltaNet triangular solve) are unreliable on this stack.
- Local full-vehicle run without a `ulimit -v` cap thrashes swap on a 30 GB box; cap it.
- NKI tracer/device rules 1-10 (`nki-tracer-rules`): #8 128-row-aligned DMAs only inside custom ops, #9 no strided OUTPUT
  DMAs, #10 custom-op Python bodies run only during tracing.
- Every new version: commit + push to `personal-remote` (memory `git-personal-remote`); never commit `trn2.pem` / `trn2-access.md`.
