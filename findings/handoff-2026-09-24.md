## The three rules that produced all of it

1. **The backward is VectorE-bound** (Vector 82% busy, Scalar 45%, Tensor 51%) and is 46-48% of the step.
   **DELETING VectorE work pays; RELOCATING it to the idle ScalarE does NOT** - a change that moved 1.8e9
   element-passes off VectorE measured exactly **0.0%**. VectorE is 2-4x faster on bf16 operands and ScalarE is
   not, and `nl.sqrt` is a slow transcendental. Relocation might pay for fp32 data; untested (the attempt was
   blocked by rule 3 below).
2. **Element-pass counting RANKS candidates but over-predicts payoff 2-4x** once the largest item is gone. It was
   right for change #1 and wrong for every later estimate (-0.8% where counting said -3.8%; the "free" outT
   emission was worth 0.15% where the profile attribution implied 0.65%). **Screen, do not project.**
3. **This file has MULTIPLE implementations of the same operation and a knob can silently reach a dead one.**
   Four instances this session: `SCAT4` (the vehicle defaults `EMBED_KERNEL=v1`, so the new kernel was
   unreachable and a guarded `try/except` hid it - the screen measured *nothing* and looked fine); `HEAD_SCALAR`;
   and `DY4T` **twice** (the clean file calls a local `attn_bwd` helper, the vehicle uses the registered op
   `tf_nki::doc_attn_bwd`, and the second `dy4` construction is the live one). **Confirm which implementation is
   live before measuring, and prefer a loud failure to a guarded fallback.**

## Measurement discipline (this is what the numbers above rest on)

- **Exchange rate: -0.0404 bpb per e-fold of steps**, measured twice this session (R_REF->G_ALL, and v48->v49).
  1% more steps ~ -0.0004 bpb. This is what makes step-time work convert to score at all.
- **The 300 s step count has +-0.8% run-to-run noise** (639 vs 644 for identical configs). Sub-1% changes need a
  cumulative trend across arms or a full gate. The *total-time* figure is noisier still: `grad_sync` moved
  0.049 -> 0.056 between two arms of one pair and inverted the sign of the comparison.
- **Gate the CLEAN file, not the vehicle.** The clean file is genuinely faster (v46: -0.0013; v49: 0.44 vs 0.47
  s/step) because the vehicle's knob branches and dead kernels are not free.
- **Verify kernel algebra in float64 torch BEFORE writing NKI.** The `head_bwd` rewrite was checked to 1e-16 on
  all five quantities first; that turned a risky rewrite into a transcription exercise.
- The run's own startup check (`fused loss == cross_entropy(head_logits)`) reported 0.00e+00 on v49 - free
  confirmation that the rewritten forward head is exact. Watch for it in every gate log.

## Closed this session, with numbers (do not re-litigate)

- **Grad accumulation / larger batch:** 262k +0.0017, 524k +0.0113. Higher LR made it *worse* (linear +0.0030,
  beyond-linear +0.0064), so sqrt-batch scaling was already right and 131072 is the optimum **in both directions**.
- **Split LR decay** (AdamW groups faster than Muon): +0.0092 at 1.5x, +0.0209 at 2.0x.
- **Closed-form (Witten-Bell) bigram confidence** from a full-corpus startup count: wash (+0.0003/+0.0006). Built
  and verified bit-identical across the sidecar and in-graph paths; the learned scalar gate already captures it.
- **MLP_RATIO 7:** +0.0069, and +22% step time for +17% hidden width - super-linear, which is the independent
  confirmation that the (H, T) hidden tensor sets the step.
- **Merging collectives: DEAD.** Measured per-collective overhead is **0.01-0.14 ms** (`CCPROBE`, 8 ranks, real
  shapes); comm is bandwidth-bound per byte. This also kills the sparse-bigram-gradient idea, which only reduces
  bytes... and note `GATHER_BF16`, which *does* reduce bytes, paid +0.6%. Reduce bytes, not call counts.
- **The weight registry already groups by SHAPE**, so q/k/v/proj are already one (48, 512, 512) stack. Do not
  propose merging them.
- **fp8** remains the wrong lever for the *right* reason now: the bottleneck is VectorE, not the PE.
- The free `outT` emission (v50): neutral (+0.15% steps, -0.00002 bpb). Rejected to keep the submission simple.
